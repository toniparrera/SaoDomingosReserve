THE ESTATE

A Rare Investment Opportunity on Portugal's Alentejo Coast

Located just 30 minutes from the Atlantic Coast and approximately 90 minutes from Lisbon and the Algarve, Monte do Pego Estate combines immediate agricultural income with a unique hospitality development opportunity in one of Portugal's most sought-after regions.

The 50.05-hectare estate benefits from a favorable prior information response from Santiago do Cacém Municipal Council, confirming the potential to develop a Rural Hotel with up to 6,000 sqm of gross construction area (or up to 4,000 sqm under the Rural Tourism / Agrotourism modality), creating an exceptional opportunity for investors seeking exposure to Portugal's boutique tourism market.

Beyond its development potential, the property already generates recurring income through cork production. The young cork oak forest is expected to significantly increase its productivity over the coming years, while the estate also benefits from annual EU agricultural subsidies.

Combining immediate revenue, long-term capital appreciation and development potential, Monte do Pego Estate represents a rare opportunity to acquire a strategic hospitality asset on the Alentejo Coast.

DEVELOPMENT OPPORTUNITY

A Hospitality Project Ready to Move Forward

Monte do Pego Estate benefits from a favorable prior information response from Santiago do Cacém Municipal Council, confirming the potential to develop a Rural Hotel with up to 6,000 sqm of gross construction area.

The property has been recognized as an independent title through a de facto division certificate issued by the Municipal Council in May 2026, providing a clear legal framework for future development. The owner is preparing the formal submission of the Prior Information Request (PIP), which will provide a binding planning decision ahead of detailed architectural design.

The project offers exceptional flexibility, allowing the creation of a luxury boutique hotel, wellness retreat, nature resort or branded hospitality concept within one of Portugal's fastest-growing tourism destinations.

Large hospitality development opportunities of this scale have become increasingly scarce along the Alentejo Coast, making Monte do Pego Estate a unique long-term investment.
